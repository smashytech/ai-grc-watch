1 · 🇪🇺 European Union · 12 Oct 2026

Commission consults on procedural rules for AI Act enforcement proceedings

Consultation  GRC impact ■■■ Medium

The Commission has published a draft implementing regulation on how it will run proceedings under Articles 75c and 75d of the AI Act. These cover AI systems built on a provider's own general-purpose model, and AI embedded in very large online platforms and search engines. The draft sets rules for access to the file after preliminary findings, protection of business secrets and digital submissions. Fines would have a five-year limitation period that restarts with each investigative step, up to an absolute cap of ten years. Feedback is open until 9 November, and adoption is planned for Q4 2026.

What it means for GRC: Together with the first enforcement requests covered in Issue No. 1, this fills in the procedure behind AI Act fines. Affected providers should note that the limitation clock can restart and should prepare confidentiality claims early. Deployers can expect suppliers to become more careful about what they disclose under contract.

2 · 🇺🇸 United States · 14 Oct 2026

State attorneys general seek child-safety conditions in Character.AI bankruptcy sale

Enforcement  GRC impact ■■■ Low

A coalition of state attorneys general, including Connecticut's, has filed a notice in the bankruptcy case of Character Technologies, the company behind Character.AI, which recently filed for Chapter 11. The states want any buyer of the business or its assets to be bound by protections for children and for the sensitive conversation data the platform holds. They point out that the company's current safeguards for minors are voluntary and a new owner could drop them. The states have reserved the right to oppose any sale or plan.

What it means for GRC: AI chat logs are now treated as high-risk assets in deals. If you buy or sell an AI product, cover conversation data and existing safety commitments in due diligence and in the data-transfer terms.
